## How do I provide liquidity?

First, you'll need to review which mining opportunities exist on TELx. All active TELx pools can be found on the [pools](/pools) page.

The pools page should look something like this:

![pools_page_1.jpg](/aboutMedia/pools_page_1.jpg)

Notice there are three pools on Polygon and two on each of Base and Ethereum. Depending on which chain your assets are located on, and which pool you would like to provide liquidity to, you may need to bridge. Check out how to bridge your assets [here](/about/how-do-i-bridge-to-polygon-pos-chain).

Once your assets are on the correct chain, follow the walkthrough below to provide liquidity to a TELx pool.

1\. Select the pool from the pools page. You will be taken to the specific pool page, for example the eUSD/ eMXN pool:
![eusd_emxn_pool_page.jpg](/aboutMedia/eusd_emxn_pool_page.jpg)
2\. Click 'Add Liquidity On Uniswap' towards the top right corner of the page. You will be taken to the Uniswap site with the pool information pre loaded for you:

![eusd_emxn_uni_page_upper.jpg](/aboutMedia/eusd_emxn_uni_page_upper.jpg)

Select your liquidity range. For a brief introduction on providing liquidity to concentrated liquidity pools, see this guide [here](/about/what-are-automated-market-makers?)
5\. Scroll down and input the number of tokens you wish to provide as liquidity. Ensure the pool price is in line with the price of the assets on the broader market.
6\. Click 'Review'.
7\. Click 'Create'.
8\. Sign the transaction using your wallet.
9\. You are now an LP on Uniswap!

In the next step, we will [walk through](/about/how-do-i-stake-my-liquidity) how you can begin mining [TELx incentives](/about/telx-market-participants-&-incentives) with your NFT position(s).
